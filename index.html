How to play

Clear every target in all 5 levels before the timer runs out. Targets fly around the arena, change direction without warning, and from level 2 they periodically raise a cyan shield – wait for the shield to drop. Hit the red bullseye centre for bonus points. Quick kills build a combo multiplier (up to x5).

Touch controls

Left thumb: virtual joystick to move. Right side: drag anywhere to aim. Big FIRE button (you can drag from it to aim while shooting). AIM toggles aim-down-sights (scope for the Kar98). Reload button, and AK / DE / K98 buttons switch weapons instantly. Arrow near the crosshair and the radar point to the nearest target.

Weapons

AK-47 – fully automatic, big 30-round magazine, climbing recoil. Best for tracking: each hit is light, so keep the crosshair on the target.
Desert Eagle – heavy single shots, slow cadence and a hard kick. Rewards confident, accurate shots.
Kar98 – bolt-action sniper: one hit destroys any target, razor-precise when scoped, but slow to cycle and very slow to reload.

Customize

Settings → Customize controls lets you drag every button to where your thumbs like it and resize them. Everything is saved on the device.
